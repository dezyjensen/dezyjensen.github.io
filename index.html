About me

A curious mind, pointed at technology

I'm a developer in Edmonton with a physics degree and a lifelong habit of asking why. I like understanding how things work, whether that's the universe, a piece of software, or the reason a system behaves the way it does.

Most of my work is building software that people rely on every day, and making it faster, steadier and safer. Over time I've found myself drawn to security: technology is at its best when people can trust it.

Outside of work you'll find me at home with my husband and our two cats, Monty and Ori, usually with a science fiction book nearby.

  1. UNTIL 2021

    Physics

    A Bachelor of Science in Physics at the University of Victoria. I studied physics because I wanted to understand how the world works. My degree had a computational focus, so a lot of it was writing code to model and analyse real systems, which is where everything that came next began.

  2. DURING UNIVERSITY

    Research

    During my physics degree I took on a research project on metamaterials: materials engineered so that their structure, not just what they're made of, gives them unusual properties, like bending light or sound in ways natural materials can't.

  3. 2023 – 2024

    Fellowship

    A fellowship at Cybera in Edmonton, where I built a machine-learning dashboard that predicts pipeline corrosion for oil and gas companies. We were careful to protect the sensitive data behind it.

  4. 2022 – NOW

    Building

    At Appsquire, a software consultancy in Edmonton, I build and look after the apps, APIs and cloud systems behind a smart hot tub platform: the things people use from their phones or ask their voice assistant to do. I like making technology quietly faster and more dependable. One of my favourite fixes cut voice-command response times in half.

  5. ALONG THE WAY

    Protecting

    Along the way I found the part I care about most: keeping people and their information safe. I've closed sign-in loopholes, cleaned secrets out of code, and rolled out security fixes carefully enough that nobody got locked out.

  6. NEXT

    Still exploring

    Right now I'm learning to build with AI agents and going deeper into cybersecurity. I'm curious about where both are heading, and how AI is going to shape our future.
